# Case template and worked type tables

Use the blank card for every new family. The three tables below are worked examples from a research pass on **iaido / quick-draw slashes**, **ground slam + fire**, and **slime slaps**. Every URL was opened during that pass. Do not invent links. Asset-pack pages are cited for *structure and timing descriptions only* — do not copy their art into a deliverable.

## Blank case card

```md
### N. Name

- URL: (the page you opened)
- One line: (what this case is useful for)
- Wind-up:
- Impact frame / hitstop:
- Main shape:
- Rings, cracks, debris, or residue:
- Fire / goo / smoke language:
- Dissipate and timing:
- Color:
- What the page actually states vs. what you are inferring:
```

Collect **8–12** cards, then 4–6 shared patterns for that family, then write the scheme.

## Type A — Iaido / quick-draw slash / 拔刀斩

Shared patterns from these cases: the wind-up is "the blade is still sheathed" plus a small glint; the cut itself is a thin line, smear, or pass-through, not a filled pie; timing is long–very short–long (often 1–3 frames of cut); the burst or delayed damage display can arrive *after* the line; sheathing is part of the move; black-and-white or split frames punctuate the hit, they do not replace the blade shape.

## Type B — Ground slam + fire / shockwave / 砸地火焰

Shared patterns: the body lifts first, then slams; the shockwave is a **hollow ring** or a traveling front, not a filled bullseye (the centre can be thinner than the ring); ground matter (cracks, stones, scorch) and fire are two layers; fire is irregular and cools on its own life (white → yellow → orange → red → smoke); timing is long lift, 1–2 frames of impact, a freeze of the peak, then a long residue; debris has gravity; particles accent the slam, they do not replace it.

| # | Case | URL | One-line drawing note |
|---|---|---|---|

| 2 | *Elden Ring* — Godfrey stomps | [Godfrey, First Elden Lord (wiki.gg)](https://eldenring.wiki.gg/wiki/Godfrey,_First_Elden_Lord) | A raised foot or axe, then rock spikes and/or a large shockwave; ground matter and the wave are separate. |
| 3 | *Dark Souls* — Smough's jumping slam | [Ornstein & Smough (wikidot)](https://darksouls.wikidot.com/dragon-slayer-ornstein-executioner-smough) | A full-body hop, then a slam whose damage falls off with distance; a later phase adds an elemental layer on the same move. |

**Scheme hint:** keep a stretch-up then squash; on frame 0 draw an irregular white core in the hitbox, a 1–2px breakable hollow ring, and flying rocks; freeze the peak with 6–10 uneven fire tongues (not a 16-petal flower); dissipate per tongue / rock / puff (white → yellow → orange → red → smoke) and leave cracks plus scorch in the hitbox. No full-screen black-and-white invert on a heavy-but-not-climax stomp unless the project asks for it.

## Type C — Slime slap / body slam / 黏液拍击

Shared patterns: the body is the weapon (squash and stretch with almost-constant area); a jump or gather comes before the squash; residual goo (decal, trail, droplets, split bodies) lasts longer than the hit flash; the face or silhouette should still be readable; mid-tier feedback stays restrained (no climax screen invert); landing squash is brief, then a wobble back.

|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | *Slime Rancher* | [A Frame of Slime Rancher](https://pixelalchemy.dev/posts/a-frame-of-slime-rancher/); [sound-design notes](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/audio/using-sound-design-to-give-i-slime-rancher-i-charm-without-getting-too-gooey) | The body keeps wobbling; a hard hit leaves a lasting splat decal, not only a one-frame flash. |
| 2 | *Terraria* — King Slime | [wiki](https://terraria.wiki.gg/wiki/King_Slime) | A readable jump loop; the landing *is* the slap; the body shrinks as it is hurt and jumps more often. |

## How to add a fourth type

Repeat the same pass: 8–12 opened URLs, one-line drawing notes, 4–6 shared patterns, then a timeline scheme. Typical next families: beam / laser, projectile burst, dash trail, summoning circle, ice shatter, lightning fork. Do not reuse a slash crescent or a fire flower just because those scripts already exist.
